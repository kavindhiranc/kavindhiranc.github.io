Privacy Policy

Keerthy Reacts Automation is a personal tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Google Drive files (video clips and assets the owner placed there) and uploads videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

How data is used

Files are processed on the owner's own server to produce videos. Video/audio content may be sent to Google's Gemini API to generate captions, titles and content checks. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's server. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

The app's use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
